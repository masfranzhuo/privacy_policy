# Privacy Policy for Jigsaw Puzzle World

Effective date: February 17, 2026

Jigsaw Puzzle World ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data is processed when you use our mobile app.

## 1. Information We Process

### A) Information stored locally on your device
The app stores gameplay data on your device using local storage (SharedPreferences), such as:
- coins and rewards
- completed levels and unlocked categories
- daily challenge claim status
- ad-free and purchase-unlock status

We use this data only to provide game functionality and save your progress.

### B) Advertising data (Google AdMob and mediation partners)
The app shows ads using Google AdMob and may use mediation partners (for example, Chartboost via AdMob mediation). These providers may process data such as:
- device identifiers (including advertising ID, where applicable)
- IP address
- device/app information
- ad performance and interaction events

This processing is handled by the ad providers under their own privacy policies.

### C) In-app purchase data (RevenueCat + app store billing)
For purchases (such as ad removal or content unlocks), we use RevenueCat and store billing services (Google Play Billing / Apple App Store). These services may process:
- purchase transaction details
- app user/device identifiers used for entitlement and receipt validation

We do not collect or store your payment card details directly.

## 2. How We Use Information
We process data to:
- run the game features and save progress
- show and manage ads
- process and restore in-app purchases
- detect issues and improve app stability/monetization performance

## 3. Data Sharing
We do not sell your personal information.  
Data may be shared with service providers only as needed to operate the app:
- Google AdMob and mediation ad networks
- RevenueCat
- Google Play / Apple App Store billing systems

## 4. Data Retention
- Local gameplay data remains on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the app.
- Third-party providers retain data according to their own policies.

## 5. Children’s Privacy
This app is designed for a general audience. If you believe a child has provided personal information in a way that violates applicable law, contact us and we will review the request.

## 6. Your Choices
- You can stop all data processing by uninstalling the app.
- You can reset or limit your advertising ID in device settings (where available).
- You can clear local app data from your device settings.
