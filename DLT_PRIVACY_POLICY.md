# Privacy Policy for Daily Luck Test

Last updated: March 17, 2026

Daily Luck Test ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the Daily Luck Test mobile application ("App") handles information.

## Summary

- We do not require account creation.
- We do not collect personal information directly through the App.
- We do not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or user tracking in the App.
- The App stores limited gameplay data locally on your device so it can show your latest result.
- If you choose to share your result, the App creates a temporary image and sends it to the sharing app you select.

## Information the App Stores

Daily Luck Test stores the following information locally on your device:

- your latest luck result
- your played date

This information is stored using local device storage and is used only to support App features, such as showing your most recent result on the home screen.

## Information We Do Not Collect

We do not intentionally collect, store, or sell:

- your name
- email address
- phone number
- precise location
- contacts
- photos
- payment information
- health information
- browsing history
- advertising identifiers for tracking

## Sharing Features

The App includes an optional feature that allows you to share your result.

When you use this feature:

- the App generates a result image temporarily on your device
- the App passes that image and share text to the system share sheet
- the selected third-party app or service (for example, a messaging or social media app) processes the shared content under its own privacy policy

We do not control how third-party apps handle content after you choose to share it.

## Permissions and Device Access

Daily Luck Test does not request sensitive runtime permissions such as access to your location, camera, microphone, or contacts.

The App may rely on standard platform capabilities needed for normal app operation and for the optional share flow.

## Data Retention

The locally stored gameplay data remains on your device until:

- you clear the App's data
- you uninstall the App
- the stored result is replaced by a newer result

Temporary share images may be created in the device's temporary storage for the share flow and may be removed by the operating system over time.

## Children's Privacy

Daily Luck Test is a simple casual game and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe the App has provided a way for personal information to be collected unexpectedly, please contact us and we will review the issue.

## Security

We aim to keep the App simple and minimize data handling. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is guaranteed to be completely secure.

## International Users

Because the App stores gameplay data locally on your device, your information generally remains on your device unless you choose to share content through another app or service.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected by revising the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
