# Privacy Policy for Football Quiz

Last updated: March 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Football Quiz (`com.molkk.football_quiz_and_trivia`) collects, uses, and shares information when you use the app.

If you publish this policy, replace the contact placeholders below with your real support email and company or developer name.

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information stored locally on your device

The app stores certain gameplay and preference data locally on your device using local app storage. This may include:

- coin balance
- daily reward progress
- ad removal and premium entitlement status cached on device
- processed purchase transaction references used to avoid duplicate rewards
- sound, vibration, and language preferences

We do not require you to create an account to use the app.

### B. Information collected by third-party services

The app uses third-party SDKs and platform services that may collect information automatically, such as:

- device identifiers
- IP address
- approximate location derived from IP address
- app interactions
- diagnostics and crash-related information
- purchase and subscription status

These data elements may be collected or processed by:

- Google AdMob, to serve ads and measure ad performance
- RevenueCat, to manage in-app purchases, entitlements, and purchase restoration
- Google Play Billing or Apple App Store billing, to process payments made through the app store

## 2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- provide core gameplay features
- save your progress and preferences on your device
- show ads, including rewarded and interstitial ads
- process in-app purchases and restore purchases
- prevent duplicate purchase rewards or entitlement errors
- improve app stability, performance, and fraud prevention

## 3. Advertising

Football Quiz uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may use device identifiers and similar information to provide, limit, personalize, or measure advertising, subject to your device settings, region, and Google policies.

If personalized advertising is offered in your region, Google may request consent or provide controls through its own systems or supported consent flows.

Learn more:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses information from sites or apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## 4. In-App Purchases

The app offers in-app purchases such as coin packs or ad removal. Purchases are processed by the app store on your device, not directly by us.

To manage entitlements and restore purchases, the app uses RevenueCat. RevenueCat may process purchase receipts, transaction identifiers, app user identifiers generated by its SDK or the platform, and subscription or entitlement status.

Learn more:

- RevenueCat Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
- Google Play Payments Policy: https://payments.google.com/payments/apis-secure/u/0/get_legal_document

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information with service providers only as needed to operate the app, including:

- Google AdMob for advertising
- RevenueCat for purchase management
- app store providers for billing and purchase restoration
- legal authorities when required by law or to protect rights, safety, or prevent fraud

## 6. Data Retention

Gameplay settings and progress stored locally remain on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the app.

Purchase-related and advertising-related information may be retained by third-party providers according to their own retention practices and legal obligations.

## 7. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, delete, or restrict certain personal information processed by third parties.

You can generally:

- stop using the app at any time
- uninstall the app
- clear app data from your device settings
- manage ad settings through your Google account or device settings
- manage purchases and subscriptions through your Google Play or Apple account

For data controlled by third-party providers, you may need to contact the relevant provider directly.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Football Quiz is intended for a general audience and is not knowingly designed to collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child has provided personal information in connection with the app, contact us and we will review the request.

## 9. Security
